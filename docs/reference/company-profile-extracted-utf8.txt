01 | COMPANY OVERVIEW
Pavana Projects
Pavana Projects is positioned as a professional Project Management Consultancy (PMC) focused on structured
planning, multidisciplinary coordination, project controls and delivery management for large-scale built-environment
projects.
Our approach is built around one objective: bringing clarity, control and accountability to complex projects
from project initiation through commissioning and handover.

06 | HOW WE MANAGE
Project Lifecycle & Delivery Methodology
Pavana Projects' methodology is designed to maintain a continuous line of sight from project objectives to final
handover.
01
INITIATE
Define objectives, scope, governance, stakeholders and project controls.
02
PLAN
Develop WBS, programme, procurement strategy, risk register and reporting systems.
03
DESIGN
Coordinate design deliverables, interfaces, constructability and approvals.
04
TENDER
Support package definition, tender coordination, evaluation and award readiness.
05
PROCURE
Track long-lead items, approvals, manufacturing, logistics and site readiness.
06
EXECUTE
Coordinate work fronts, contractors, interfaces, quality and safety.
07
COMMISSION
Drive testing, integrated systems readiness, snag closure and documentation.
08
HANDOVER
Coordinate as-builts, O&M; documents, training, statutory records and final handover.
